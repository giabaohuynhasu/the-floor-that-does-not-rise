Cybersecurity vulnerability discovery has accelerated from 16,508 annual CVEs in 2018 to over 39,500 in 2024, accompanied by a decentralization of assigning authority (active CNAs expanded from 100 to over 410, driving the CNA Herfindahl-Hirschman Index down from 1,500 to 710). 

However, administrative remediation throughput remains decoupled from discovery velocity. While CISA's Binding Operational Directive (BOD 22-01) instituted mandatory federal remediation windows (typically 14 to 21 days for KEV entries), empirical compliance data demonstrate that policy deadlines represent an administrative pacing mechanism rather than realized technical remediation. The discovery proxy cannot be converted into an administrative throughput proxy without independent auditing of organizational patching completion.
