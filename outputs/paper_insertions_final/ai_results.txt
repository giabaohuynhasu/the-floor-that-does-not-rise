The AI capability result is based on raw task-level outcomes from METR's public evaluation repository and a weighted logistic reconstruction of model-specific task horizons (T_50,m). The fitted long-run trend yields an estimated capability doubling time of approximately 7.2 months (95% CI: 5.4 to 10.8 months, R^2 = 0.81). 

However, raw residual diagnostics reveal persistent consecutive runs of negative residuals (maximum negative run = 4 evaluations), and the cumulative sum of residuals (CUSUM) indicates that apparent trend curvature is sensitive to a small cluster of recent frontier models. Furthermore, a quadratic curvature test (t^2) fails to reject the null hypothesis of log-linear constancy at the 5% level after controlling for scaffold configurations and model families. Consequently, no claim of sustained super-exponential acceleration is empirically warranted from the task-horizon data alone.
