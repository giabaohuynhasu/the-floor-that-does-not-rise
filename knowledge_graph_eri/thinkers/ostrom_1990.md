# Elinor Ostrom (1990)

**Tradition**: Institutional Economics / Common-Pool Resource Governance  
**Primary Reference**: *Governing the Commons: The Evolution of Institutions for Collective Action*

## Contribution to ERI Framework (from NotebookLM Grounding)
Ostrom established 8 design principles for long-enduring common-pool resource (CPR) institutions, demonstrating that communities can avoid the 'tragedy of the commons' through polycentric monitoring and graduated sanctions.

## ERI Dialectic / Critique / Extension
ERI adapts Ostrom's 8 design principles to political philosophy: legitimacy and sovereignty are treated as an 'institutional common-pool resource'. Volume III shows that entropy-resistant institutions survive by implementing Ostrom-style nested enterprises and graduated monitoring over executive power.
