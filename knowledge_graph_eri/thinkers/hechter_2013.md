# Michael Hechter (2013)

**Tradition**: Rational Choice Institutionalism / Political Sociology  
**Primary Reference**: *Alien Rule (Cambridge University Press)*

## Contribution to ERI Framework (from NotebookLM Grounding)
Hechter provides the micro-foundational rational choice model of indirect rule: central rulers employ local intermediaries to minimize administrative costs, but incur massive monitoring costs and 'subordinate despotism'.

## ERI Dialectic / Critique / Extension
ERI uses Hechter's rational choice logic to explain the micro-level failure of Volume I's indirect rule cases: local intermediaries use their monopoly on local information to entrench corrupt customary practices, permanently degrading the postcolonial bureaucratic floor.
