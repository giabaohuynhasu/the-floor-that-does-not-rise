# Robert K. Merton (1957)

**Tradition**: Sociology of Science  
**Primary Reference**: *Priority in Scientific Discovery: A Chapter in the Sociology of Science*

## Contribution to WCP Framework (NotebookLM Grounded)
Merton analyzed why priority of discovery is so fiercely contested in science, showing that recognition and priority are the fundamental currency of scientific institutions.

## WCP Dialectic / Critique / Extension
WCP uses Merton to ground 'Corpus Temporalism' (Vol III): scientific integrity requires verifiable sequence; priority is not just about prestige, but about proving that a model was predictive rather than retrofitted.
