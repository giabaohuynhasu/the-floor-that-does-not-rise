# Simone Weil (1940)

**Tradition**: Moral Philosophy / Political Theology  
**Primary Reference**: *The Iliad, or the Poem of Force*

## Contribution to WCP Framework (NotebookLM Grounded)
Weil defined 'force' as that x that turns anybody who is subjected to it into a thing, arguing that nobody truly possesses force; all are ultimately crushed by it.

## WCP Dialectic / Critique / Extension
WCP uses Weil to define the stance of 'The Embedded Witness' (Vol I): the researcher does not observe violence or technological disruption from above, but recognizes their own vulnerability to the mechanical forces of the era.
