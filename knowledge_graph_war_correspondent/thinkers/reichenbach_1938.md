# Hans Reichenbach (1938)

**Tradition**: Logical Empiricism / Philosophy of Science  
**Primary Reference**: *Experience and Prediction: An Analysis of the Foundations and the Structure of Knowledge*

## Contribution to WCP Framework (NotebookLM Grounded)
Reichenbach introduced the foundational distinction between the 'context of discovery' (the psychological origin of an idea) and the 'context of justification' (the formal, logical validation of an idea).

## WCP Dialectic / Critique / Extension
WCP adopts Reichenbach's context of justification for its formal truth tables, but shows that in human-AI collaboration, the context of discovery (prompts, raw data logs) must also be preserved to prevent synthetic hallucination.
