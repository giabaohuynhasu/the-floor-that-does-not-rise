# Imre Lakatos (1970)

**Tradition**: Philosophy of Science / Methodology of Research Programmes  
**Primary Reference**: *Falsification and the Methodology of Scientific Research Programmes*

## Contribution to WCP Framework (NotebookLM Grounded)
Lakatos refined Popperian falsification by introducing 'scientific research programmes' consisting of a hard core, a protective belt of auxiliary hypotheses, and positive/negative heuristics.

## WCP Dialectic / Critique / Extension
WCP adopts Lakatos's distinction between progressive and degenerating problem shifts, but requires that the protective belt must not become an impenetrable shield against empirical falsification.
