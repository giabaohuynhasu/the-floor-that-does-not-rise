# SECTION 2: THE 13 MATHEMATICAL QUEUEING MODELS & THEORETICAL FRAMEWORK

The theoretical foundation is built on non-stationary queueing systems, drawing upon Ward Whitt's $M_t/G/1$ approximations, heavy-traffic limit theorems, and state-dependent arrival/service rate dynamics.

### Model 1: Non-Stationary Arrival Rate of Synthetic Workload $\lambda(t)$
$$\lambda(t) = \lambda_0 \cdot \exp\left(\int_0^t \alpha(s) \, ds\right) + \sum_{k=1}^K \delta_k(t - t_k)$$
Synthetic workload arrival is modeled as a non-stationary Poisson/Hawkes process characterized by autonomous generation jumps $\delta_k$ at model release epochs $t_k$.

### Model 2: The Bounded Human/Institutional Service Rate $\mu(t)$
$$\mu(t) = \min\left(\mu_{max}, \frac{\mu_0}{1 + \gamma \cdot D(t)}\right)$$
Service rate $\mu(t)$ (human cognitive verification, judicial review, code auditing) is strictly bounded by human metabolic limits $\mu_{max}$ and degrades under backlog fatigue $D(t)$.

### Model 3: The Utilization Ratio & Divergence Threshold $\rho(t)$
$$\rho(t) = \frac{\lambda(t)}{c \cdot \mu(t)}$$
When $\rho(t) \to 1$, queue length and waiting times diverge asymptotically. In institutional settings where $c$ (certified human experts) is static, $\rho(t) > 1$ leads to unbounded queue accumulation (the "Cognitive Inundation Regime").

### Model 4: Expected Queue Length via Heavy-Traffic Approximation (Whitt 2002)
$$L_q(t) \approx \frac{\rho(t)^2 (c_a^2 + c_s^2)}{2(1 - \rho(t))} + \int_0^t [\lambda(s) - c\mu(s)]^+ \, ds$$
Captures the accumulation of unverified synthetic assets during heavy-traffic bursts.

### Model 5: "The Floor That Does Not Rise" (Minimum Latency Invariant $\tau_{min}$)
$$\mathbb{E}[W_q(t)] \ge \tau_{min} > 0$$
Regardless of synthetic computation speed ($\Delta t_{compute} \to 0$), institutional validation requires irreducible deliberation time $\tau_{min}$ (peer review, democratic consensus, judicial scrutiny).

### Model 6: Delayed Feedback & Control Instability
$$\frac{dx(t)}{dt} = f(x(t)) - g(x(t - \tau_{audit}))$$
Time delays $\tau_{audit}$ between capability deployment and safety auditing introduce limit cycles and chaotic regime bifurcations.

### Model 7: The Vulnerability-Patching Queue (KEV Metric)
