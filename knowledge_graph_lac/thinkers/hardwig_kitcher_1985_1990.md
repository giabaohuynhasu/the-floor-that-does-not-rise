# John Hardwig & Philip Kitcher (1985 / 1990)

**Tradition**: Social Epistemology / Philosophy of Science  
**Primary Reference**: *Epistemic Dependence (Hardwig 1985) & The Division of Cognitive Labor (Kitcher 1990)*

## Contribution to LAC Framework (NotebookLM Grounded)
Hardwig and Kitcher proved that modern science requires researchers to trust results they cannot individually verify, creating structural epistemic dependence.

## LAC Dialectic / Critique / Extension
LAC extends Hardwig and Kitcher to machine-speed science: when AI produces biogerontological hypotheses faster than human scientists can verify them, epistemic dependence shifts from human peers to machine oracles, creating systemic vulnerability to unverified biological zero-days.
