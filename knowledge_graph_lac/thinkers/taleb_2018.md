# Nassim Nicholas Taleb (2018)

**Tradition**: Mathematical Epistemology / Risk Philosophy  
**Primary Reference**: *Skin in the Game: Hidden Asymmetries in Daily Life*

## Contribution to LAC Framework (NotebookLM Grounded)
Taleb argues that systems only achieve structural stability when decision-makers have 'skin in the game'—bearing personal downside risk for their decisions.

## LAC Dialectic / Critique / Extension
LAC inverts and deepens Taleb via 'The Symmetric Wound': in longevity governance, policymakers have *infinite* skin in the game (their own families' mortality), which makes impartial public interest regulation a game-theoretic impossibility.
